Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 13, 2026

This policy explains how KEYME PTE. LTD. ("we", "us") handles information in the CardPress mobile app and on cardpress.diy and www.cardpress.diy.

Data on your phone and card

CardPress works without an account, advertising analytics or cloud synchronization. Its current mobile clients do not send your card contents, management keys, PINs or operation history to us. The app communicates directly with your card over NFC. Data written to the card remains on the card and is separate from the app's local storage.

The app stores card profiles, management keysets, observations and operation records in an encrypted local database. The database unlock key is protected using the device's Keychain on iOS or Keystore on Android. Local CardPress data is excluded from the app's system backup configuration. Encryption reduces risk but does not guarantee protection against every device compromise or loss.

Permissions and files you choose

NFC access connects to a card for actions you request. Optional camera access scans QR codes or printed information, including management keys and OTP accounts. Camera processing occurs on your device; CardPress does not upload the camera feed to us. You can manage camera permission in system settings.

Files you import, export or share use the locations and services you select. Exported recovery files, diagnostics or screenshots may contain sensitive information. The destination, including a cloud drive or messaging app, processes them under its own policies. Keep recovery files and their recovery phrases separately and review diagnostics before sharing them.

This website

The website serves static pages, images and videos. It does not use advertising trackers or an analytics SDK. If you choose a language, the browser may save the value "zh" or "en" in local storage under "cardpress-language". This preference stays in that browser and can be removed by clearing this site's storage.

Our hosting provider (Amazon Web Services) and delivery/security provider (Cloudflare) process request metadata needed to deliver and protect the website, such as IP addresses, requested URLs, browser information and request times. Our origin server records limited access and error logs for operation and security, with daily rotation and a seven-day retention setting. Security investigations may require keeping relevant records longer. Provider-operated logs, network diagnostics and security cookies are subject to those providers' policies and settings; this is separate from the mobile app's local data.

Website delivery and support may involve processing outside your country. We do not sell your personal information or use it for targeted advertising. Links to external documentation, applet projects and download services take you to services governed by their own privacy policies.

Support, retention and your choices

If you contact us, we receive your email address and any information you include. We use it to answer your enquiry and address support or security issues, retaining it only as needed for those purposes and applicable legal obligations. Do not email keys, PINs, recovery phrases or unredacted sensitive diagnostics.

You control your locally stored app data. Removing the app removes its local data subject to operating-system behavior, but does not erase your card or copies you exported elsewhere. Erasing an applet or resetting a card is a separate, potentially irreversible action. Preserve any required recovery material before removing data.

For information we hold, you may contact us to request access, correction or deletion, or to raise a privacy concern. We may need to verify a request and consider applicable legal requirements. We cannot recover or remotely erase data held only on your phone or card.

Children and changes

CardPress is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us so we can investigate and address it. We will publish changes to this policy here with an updated effective date.
