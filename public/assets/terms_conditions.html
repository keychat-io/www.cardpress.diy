What CardPress does

CardPress connects a phone to compatible Java Card smart cards through NFC. It helps inspect cards, manage applets and management keys, import CAP files, and perform installation or removal with result verification. Availability depends on your phone, operating system, card configuration, applet requirements and permissions. A catalog entry or demonstration does not guarantee compatibility with your card. Android is currently a development preview.

Applet features such as passkeys, OTP, secret storage or signing may require separate companion software. CardPress does not operate the third-party services those applets access, and does not guarantee their availability, certification or fitness for a particular purpose.

Card operations can be irreversible

Removing an applet, deleting its package, changing management keys or resetting a card can permanently erase data or prevent access. Removing CardPress from your phone does not uninstall applets or erase data on the card. Installing an applet again does not restore its previous data.

A lost NFC connection or interrupted command may leave the result uncertain. Keep the card steady, follow the app's recovery instructions and verify the saved operation against the card before another change. A displayed error does not prove that the card was unchanged. Software verification cannot reverse a card write or guarantee data recovery.
